How it works

Practice the words
before you need them

Under the hood is Marshall Rosenberg's non-violent communication: observation, feeling, need, request. The goal is not to win the argument — it is to hear the person and what stands behind their words.

🧱

See the wall move

The other person has a state — Wall and Contact. Every line you say shifts it, and you see why.

🧩

Build your own phrase

In the builder stories you assemble a line from four parts. The weakest part decides how the whole sentence lands.

⏪

Rewind time

Went wrong? Go back to any moment and try another path. Every story has four endings.

🌬️

Breathe first

While you're boiling, the most precise words are out of reach — until you calm your own anger.
